Logo system

One pixel fox, four contexts. The mark always renders with crisp edges: never anti-alias, blur, or smooth the pixels.

Primarygradient on light
Reversedgradient on ground #11110F
One-color inkplum #32122F, print & embossing
Knockoutcream fox on brand gradient
Scale in whole-pixel multiples (20, 34, 54, 64, 84, 96 px) so the grid stays sharp.
Pair the mark with the Geist wordmark at 780 weight, -0.045 em tracking.
Never rotate, skew, outline, or redraw the fox in rounded or smooth vector style.
Never recolor the gradient, apply drop shadows to the mark, or set the wordmark in gradient fills.
Never place the gradient mark on mid-tone or clashing photographic backgrounds; use the ink or knockout variant.

Color

A warm ember gradient carries the brand; a near-black warm ground carries the product. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Brand gradient

135° linear. Reserved for the fox mark, the app icon, and one hero moment per surface. Never used for text.

Core palette

Product surface — dark

The app lives on a warm near-black. Semantic colors are muted and warm-shifted so evidence states read calmly, not like alarms.

Typography

Geist carries every register; Geist Mono is reserved for evidence: counts, scores, hashes, and code. Two families, no more.

Verification voice
